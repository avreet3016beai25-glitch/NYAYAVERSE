## 🌟 Executive Summary

**NYAYAVERSE** is a revolutionary, futuristic, AI-powered consumer protection platform for India. Built to transform the relationship between 1.4 billion citizens and deceptive commercial practices, NYAYAVERSE combines artificial intelligence, cybersecurity telemetry, financial forensics, interactive simulations, and statutory legal awareness under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019**.

Rather than a static informational portal, NYAYAVERSE operates like an **AI Command Centre**, equipping citizens with the investigative intelligence to detect dark patterns, dismantle illegal overcharging, survive online scams, adjudicate virtual courtroom trials, and generate formal legal notices in 60 seconds.

## 🛸 Key Features & Interactive Architecture

### 1. 🛡️ Command Centre (Homepage)
- **3D Animated Justice Shield & Canvas Particles**: Real-time rotating holographic rings and particle bursts generated via HTML5 Canvas.
- **Live Consumer Threat Monitor**: Real-time simulated threat telemetry feed highlighting active deceptive patterns across Indian cities (clearly marked as demonstration data).
- **Protection Telemetry Gauges**: Consumer Protection Readiness Score (88/100), 1.48M+ demo reported incidents, and ₹42.8 Cr simulated citizen savings.
- **Massive Interactive Problem Search**: Instant query router *"What happened to you today?"* routing common grievances (*Overcharged above MRP, fake product, rejected refund, fake discount, misleading ad*) directly to corresponding forensic tools.

### 2. 🔍 Truth Scanner AI (The Signature Feature)
- **Multi-Format Ingestion**: Upload shopping screenshots, restaurant/cafe bills, product labels, e-commerce offers, or customer rejection emails.
- **6 Built-in Deep Real-World Presets**:
  1. *Cafe Overcharging on Packaged Water*: ₹65 charged on ₹20 printed MRP + 10% forced service charge.
  2. *E-Commerce Fake 90% Flash Sale*: Artificial urgency countdown looping timer + inflated baseline MRP.
  3. *Ayurvedic "100% Diabetes Cure" Label*: Prohibited disease cure claim violating Drugs & Magic Remedies Act + missing FSSAI.
  4. *Airline Drip Pricing*: Search fare ₹3,200 inflating to ₹5,478 via pre-ticked travel insurance and sneak-in fees.
  5. *Electronics "Warranty Void if Seal Broken" Notice*: Unfair contract term under CPA 2019 Section 2(46).
  6. *EdTech "100% Placement or Refund" Contract*: Unconscionable qualification hurdles.
- **The Signature Truth Report**:
  - Risk Level Gauge (*Low, Moderate, High, Critical*) with numeric score.
  - Detected Red Flags with severity chips and exact statutory sections.
  - Extracted Evidence Line-Item Table.
  - Statutory Legal Analysis.
  - Recommended Next Steps & Direct one-click transfer into Complaint Generator.

### 3. 🤖 AI Investigator (Nyaya AI)
- **Conversational Legal Assistant**: Plain-language dialogue explaining complex Indian statutes simply.
- **Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech**: Hands-free voice problem description (Web Speech API) and AI speech reading.
- **Preservation Checklist**: Generates essential evidence checklists (invoices, WhatsApp logs, uncut unboxing videos).
- **Structured 5-Step Action Plan**: Step-by-step roadmap from internal grievance officer to NCH 1915 and e-Daakhil.
- **Engine Transparency**: Transparently reflects whether responses are powered by live Google Gemini models (when user supplies key in settings) or the built-in Indian Consumer Legal Reasoning Engine.

### 4. 🏛️ AI Courtroom (Virtual Redressal Bench)
- **Adjudicate 5 Realistic Indian Disputes**:
  - *The Unboxing Disaster*: Swapped phone inside sealed box; seller claims OTP delivery waives inspection.
  - *The ₹70 Airport Cafe Water Bottle*: Takeaway packaged water charged above printed MRP ₹20.
  - *The Ghost Fitness Subscription*: Trial cancelled on Day 4, but app debited ₹2,999/mo for 5 months.
  - *The Miracle Acne Cream*: Unlisted topical steroid causing severe chemical burns.
  - *The 95% Flight Cancellation Fee*: Airline confiscating 95% of ticket price under "free cancellation promo".
- **6-Round Interactive Hearing**:
  - Round 1: Undisputed Case Facts
  - Round 2: Forensic Evidence Exhibits (IMEI certificates, lab reports, credit notes)
  - Round 3: Consumer's Testimony
  - Round 4: Seller's Defense & Contract Clauses
  - Round 5: User Acts as Judge (Casting judicial decision)
  - Round 6: Official Commission Verdict with synthesized Gavel Strike sound, NCDRC precedents, and real-life takeaways.

### 5. 🗺️ Consumer Radar India (Geospatial Intelligence)
- **Tactical SVG Map of India**: Interactive hotspots across 28+ states and union territories.
- **Multi-Category Telemetry**: Online Shopping, Fake Products, Overcharging MRP, Banking & Payments, Misleading Ads, Food Safety.
- **Filters**: By State, Category, and Verification Status (*Verified by Nyaya, Under Investigation, Community Reported*).
- **Anonymous Incident Reporting**: Interactive modal allowing users to report local consumer exploitation, instantly plotting it onto the tactical radar.

### 6. 🔥 Scam Survival Arena (Interactive Cyber Game)
- **Predatory Store Simulation ("FlashLoot Bazaar India")**:
  - ₹10,000 Starting Virtual Wallet.
  - 120-second countdown timer.
  - 7 Active Traps: False Urgency clock, Fake 92.5% discount, Bot reviews, Drip pricing at checkout, Phishing UPI redirect, Fake government trust seal, and OTP fraud prompt.
  - Click to "Flag Trap" (+150 XP) or suffer wallet penalties if falling for tricks.
  - 3 Difficulty Tiers (*Cadet, Agent, Vigilante*).
  - Final Scam Survival Score & learning debrief.

### 7. 📊 Money Detective (Financial Forensic Calculator)
- Calculates true effective discounts vs illusory discounts.
- Detects hidden drip fees, surge pricing, and packaging surcharges.
- Compares initial advertised price against checkout total.
- Nuanced statutory evaluation distinguishing between illegal MRP violations (Legal Metrology Rule 18(2)), deceptive drip pricing (CCPA Dark Patterns 2023), and permissible commercial markups.

### 8. ⚡ Justice in 60 Seconds (Complaint Generator)
- 4-step wizard composing formal **Pre-Litigation Legal Notices** and **DCDRC Complaint Drafts**.
- Automatic citation of relevant sections of the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (Sections 2(47), 2(11), 84-87).
- Evidence checklist generator.
- 4-Tier Escalation Roadmap (*Internal Grievance -> NCH 1915 -> e-Daakhil -> CCPA*).
- Actions: One-click Copy to clipboard, Print-ready view, and `.txt` file export.

### 9. 🌌 Consumer Rights Universe
- Holographic explorer for the 6 statutory consumer rights under CPA 2019:
  1. *Right to Safety*
  2. *Right to be Informed*
  3. *Right to Choose*
  4. *Right to be Heard*
  5. *Right to Redressal*
  6. *Right to Consumer Education*
- Real-life situations, landmark Indian judgments (Supreme Court & NCDRC), and interactive **Myth vs Fact** challenge cards.

### 10. 👤 Personal Consumer Dashboard
- Live Consumer IQ Score & Level (*Nyaya Sentinel, Guardian, Investigator*).
- Calculated / simulated capital protected.
- Complete scan history, courtroom trials won, and scam survival XP.
- Archive of drafted legal notices.
- Unlockable achievement badges persisted in browser `localStorage`.
